Assemblies Of God,Mozambique

Assemblies of God in Mozambique is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities throughout Mozambique. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship lists the Igreja Evangélica Assembleia de Deus in Mozambique among its participating national bodies.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Mozambique developed within the broader Pentecostal missionary movement in Southern and southeastern Africa. The work grew through evangelism and the establishment of local Christian congregations.


History

As the movement developed, local churches and Christian leaders became central to its ministry. Portuguese-speaking Christian communities provided an important context for the development of the church, while local leadership helped carry the work into additional communities.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development and Christian service.


Today

AG Mozambique continues its ministry through local congregations and Christian leaders, seeking to proclaim Christ and strengthen Christian communities across the country. Its national church is based in Maputo.
