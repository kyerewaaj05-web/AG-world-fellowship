Assemblies Of God,Tanzania

The Tanzania Assemblies of God is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities throughout Tanzania. The national body is known as the General Council of the Tanzania Assemblies of God, and it is represented within the World Assemblies of God Fellowship.


Origin

The Assemblies of God movement in Tanzania developed within the wider Pentecostal missionary expansion in East Africa. The work grew through evangelism, local Christian communities and the development of Tanzanian leadership.


History

Over the years, the church developed congregations and ministries in different parts of Tanzania. Leadership development and evangelism became important parts of its continuing growth, while the national church also became involved in wider Assemblies of God fellowship and mission.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, planting churches, training leaders and reaching communities with the message of Christ.


Today

Tanzania Assemblies of God continues to operate through local churches and Christian ministries, with its national leadership based in Dar es Salaam
