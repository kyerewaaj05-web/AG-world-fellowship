Assemblies of God,Kyrgyzstan

The Assemblies of God in Kyrgyzstan is part of the international Pentecostal movement. Its ministry includes local churches, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian teaching.


Origin

Assemblies of God ministry in Kyrgyzstan developed within the Pentecostal work of the former Soviet region. Assemblies of God sources record that the movement initially included mainly Russian members, while U.S. Assemblies of God workers arrived in 1995 and began ministry among the Kyrgyz people.


History

The movement developed during the period following the Soviet era, when new opportunities opened for Christian evangelism and international missionary work.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers, establish churches and equip Christians for ministry.


Today

The Assemblies of God continues to maintain a Pentecostal presence in Kyrgyzstan while serving within the country's Central Asian context.
