Assemblies of God,Guatemala

The Assemblies of God in Guatemala form part of the large Pentecostal movement in Central America.


Origin

Assemblies of God ministry developed through Pentecostal evangelism and missionary activity, followed by the establishment and expansion of local Guatemalan churches.


History

The movement expanded through evangelism, church planting, preaching and discipleship, reaching communities in different parts of the country.


Mission

The mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, establish churches and equip Christian leaders.


Today

WAGF currently lists Asambleas de Dios en Guatemala as its participating Assemblies of God organization for Guatemala.
