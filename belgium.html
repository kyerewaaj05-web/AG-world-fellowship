Assemblies of God,Belgium

The Assemblies of God presence in Belgium includes Pentecostal communities serving both Dutch-speaking and French-speaking populations.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Belgium through the wider European Pentecostal movement, with congregations emerging among different linguistic communities.


History

Over time, Belgian Pentecostal organizations developed structures for cooperation, leadership and church ministry. WAGF's directory currently records separate Flemish and French-speaking Belgian organizations.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim Christ, disciple believers, establish healthy congregations and reach Belgium's diverse communities.


Today

Belgium is represented in the WAGF directory through both Flemish and French-speaking Pentecostal organizations.
