Assemblies Of God,Uganda

Assemblies of God in Uganda is a Pentecostal Christian movement committed to proclaiming Jesus Christ, strengthening believers and developing churches throughout the country. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship currently lists both Uganda Assemblies of God and Pentecostal Assemblies of God - Uganda among its participating bodies.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Uganda developed within the wider Pentecostal expansion in East Africa. The work grew through evangelism, Christian fellowship, local congregations and the development of Ugandan Christian leadership.


History

As Pentecostal ministry developed in Uganda, local churches and ministers became increasingly involved in carrying the work forward. The movement developed a national presence centred around worship, evangelism, discipleship and leadership development.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, establish churches, train Christian leaders and serve communities.


Today

AG ministry in Uganda continues through local churches, pastors and Christian workers, seeking to strengthen believers and reach more communities with the Gospel.
