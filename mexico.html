Assemblies of God, Mexico

The Assemblies of God in Mexico is a large Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through churches, evangelism, discipleship, Christian education, leadership development and missions.


Origin

The movement developed as Pentecostal Christianity expanded throughout Mexico during the twentieth century.


History

Local churches grew into an organized national movement known as the Concilio Nacional de las Asambleas de Dios.


Mission

Its mission includes preaching the Gospel, making disciples, planting churches, training ministers and extending Christian ministry throughout Mexico.


Today

The Concilio Nacional de las Asambleas de Dios is listed by WAGF as Mexico's national Assemblies of God body, with its headquarters in Mexico City.
