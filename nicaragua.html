Assemblies of God, Nicaragua

The Assemblies of God in Nicaragua is a major Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through churches, evangelism, discipleship, Bible education and missions.


Origin

The movement developed as Pentecostal Christianity spread through Central America.


History

The Nicaraguan Assemblies of God developed an extensive network of churches, pastors, evangelists and Bible-training institutions.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, ministerial education and cross-cultural missions.


Today

WAGF's profile reports 1,164 organized churches, 87 church plants, 1,893 pastors, 80 lay ministers, 120 evangelists and approximately 646,663 members, along with about 3,500 students in biblical institutes. It also records Nicaraguan missionaries serving in several countries.
