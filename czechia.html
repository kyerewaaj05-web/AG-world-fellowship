Assemblies of God, Czechia

The Assemblies of God presence in Czechia is represented within the country's Pentecostal movement and is connected with the wider World Assemblies of God Fellowship. WAGF lists the Apostolic Church as the participating Pentecostal body for Czechia.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in the Czech lands during the twentieth century as Pentecostal teaching and ministry spread through Central Europe.


History

The movement developed through local congregations, Christian workers and cooperation with Pentecostal churches in neighboring European countries. Over time, national leadership and organized church structures were established.


Mission

Its mission centers on proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, establishing churches, biblical teaching, prayer and equipping believers for Christian service.


Today

The Apostolic Church is listed by WAGF as the national Pentecostal body representing Czechia, with its leadership based in Kolín.
