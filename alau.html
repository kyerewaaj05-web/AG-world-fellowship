Assemblies of God, Palau

The Assemblies of God of Palau is served by the General Council of the Assemblies of God of the Northern Mariana and Palau Islands, which oversees the Pentecostal fellowship in Palau as part of its regional fellowship affiliated with the World Assemblies of God Fellowship.


Origin

Pentecostal ministry developed through evangelistic and missionary work in the Pacific, followed by the development of local congregations.


History

Local churches developed through preaching, worship, discipleship and community ministry. Ministry has included evangelism, Bible teaching, youth and family ministry and leadership development.


Mission

Ministry has included evangelism, Bible teaching, youth and family ministry and leadership development.


Today

Palau belongs to the Oceania/Pacific portion of the worldwide Assemblies of God network, served by the regional fellowship headquartered in Saipan.
