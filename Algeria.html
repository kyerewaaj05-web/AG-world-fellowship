Assemblies of God,Algeria

Assemblies of God Ghana is a Pentecostal Christian movement whose roots in Ghana date to 1931, when pioneer missionaries established work in Yendi. Over the decades, the movement developed into a nationwide church involving congregations, ministers, missionaries and various ministries.


Origin

The Assemblies of God mission in Ghana originated through the work of American missionaries Lloyd and Margaret Shirer, Guy Hickok and Beulah Buchwalter. They entered the Gold Coast in 1931 and began their work in Yendi.


History

From its beginnings in northern Ghana, the movement gradually expanded into other parts of the country. Ghanaian believers increasingly participated in ministry and leadership, and the mission developed into an established national church.


Growth & Development

The church's development has included evangelism, church planting, discipleship, leadership training, education, community development and missions. Ghanaian ministers and congregations have played an increasingly important role in carrying the work forward.


Mission

Today, Assemblies of God Ghana continues its ministry through local congregations, evangelism, discipleship, missions, leadership development and community-oriented ministries.
