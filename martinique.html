Assemblies of God, Martinique

The Assemblées de Dieu de Martinique is the Pentecostal fellowship in Martinique affiliated with the World Assemblies of God Fellowship, serving churches across the French Caribbean territory.


Origin

Pentecostal ministry developed through evangelistic and missionary work in the Caribbean, followed by the development of local congregations.


History

Churches expanded through preaching, worship, evangelism and discipleship. Local ministries developed around congregational life, youth, families, prayer and Christian education.


Mission

The mission is to proclaim the Gospel and strengthen Christian communities throughout Martinique.


Today

Martinique is treated as a distinct location in the wider WAGF directory framework rather than being automatically absorbed into a sovereign-country list. The national fellowship serves churches across the territory with its headquarters in Ducos.
