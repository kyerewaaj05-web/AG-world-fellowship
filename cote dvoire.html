Assemblies Of God,Ivory Coast-Côte d'Ivoire

The Église Évangélique des Assemblées de Dieu de Côte d'Ivoire is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities throughout Côte d'Ivoire. It is represented within the World Assemblies of God Fellowship.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Côte d'Ivoire developed as Pentecostal Christianity expanded through West Africa. Missionary work, evangelism and local Christian participation contributed to the establishment of Assemblies of God congregations.


History

The church developed through the establishment of local congregations and the training of Christian leaders. As the movement grew, Ivorian ministers and believers became increasingly responsible for its continuing development.


Mission

The church's mission includes proclaiming Jesus Christ, making disciples, planting churches, training leaders and serving communities.


Today

AG Côte d'Ivoire continues its ministry through local churches and Christian workers, seeking to extend the Gospel and strengthen Christian communities throughout the country.
