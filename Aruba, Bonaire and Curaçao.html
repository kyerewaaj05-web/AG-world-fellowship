Assemblies of God, Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao

Assemblies of God ministry in Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao is represented by the Assemblies of God in Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence developed within the Pentecostal Christian movement of the Caribbean islands.


History

The three islands have been represented together within the WAGF directory under one Assemblies of God leadership structure.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, establish and stre ngthen churches, and equip believers for Christian service.


Today

WAGF lists Rev. Kenneth Thijm as General Superintendent for the Assemblies of God in Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao.
