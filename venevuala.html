Assemblies of God,Venezuela

The Assemblies of God in Venezuela is represented by Asambleas de Dios de Venezuela.


Origin

The movement developed within Venezuela's Pentecostal Christian community.


History

Assemblies of God churches have established a national ministry structure serving communities throughout Venezuela.


Mission

Its ministry centers on Gospel proclamation, discipleship, church development and Christian service.


Today

WAGF lists Asambleas de Dios de Venezuela as the country's participating Assemblies of God organization, with its national leadership based in Caracas.
