Assemblies of God,Sierra Leone

The Sierra Leone Assemblies of God is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving Sierra Leone through local churches, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian ministry. It is represented within the worldwide Assemblies of God fellowship.


Origin

The movement developed as Pentecostal Christianity spread through West Africa, eventually establishing an organized Assemblies of God presence in Sierra Leone.


History

As the movement developed, local Christian leadership and congregations became central to its continuing work. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship recognizes the Sierra Leone Assemblies of God as a national participating body, with its leadership based in Freetown.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, establish and strengthen churches, and equip believers for Christian service./p>

Today

Today, the Sierra Leone Assemblies of God continues its ministry under national leadership in Freetown and remains connected to the wider World Assemblies of God Fellowship.
