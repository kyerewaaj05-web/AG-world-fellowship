Assemblies of God,Armenia

The Assemblies of God presence in Armenia is represented through Pentecostal and evangelical churches participating in the wider World Assemblies of God Fellowship.


Origin

The movement developed within Armenia's evangelical and Pentecostal Christian community.


History

WAGF currently lists several Armenian Pentecostal/evangelical bodies, including the Union of Evangelical Faith Churches of the Republic of Armenia, the Unity Church of Christ of the Gospel Faith, and the Vernatun Evangelical Church in Yerevan.


Mission

Their ministry centers on Christian evangelism, discipleship, church development and the strengthening of evangelical and Pentecostal communities.


Today

Armenia's WAGF representation therefore consists of several participating Pentecostal/evangelical organizations rather than a single national Assemblies of God body.
