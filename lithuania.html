Assemblies of God,Lithuania

The Pentecostal movement in Lithuania is represented by the Union of Pentecostal Churches of Lithuania, which participates in the wider international Pentecostal fellowship.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Lithuania during the twentieth century, with significant growth occurring as religious restrictions changed after the Soviet period.


History

Pentecostal churches developed through local congregations and Christian leaders. Bible-training initiatives also contributed to Pentecostal development in Lithuania; WAGF missions material records a Bible school operating there during the 1990s.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, biblical teaching and leadership development.


Today

The Union of Pentecostal Churches of Lithuania is listed by WAGF, with its leadership based in Vilnius.
