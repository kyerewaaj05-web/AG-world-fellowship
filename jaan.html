Assemblies of God,Japan

The Japan Assemblies of God is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities throughout Japan through churches, evangelism, discipleship, worship, Christian teaching and missions.


Origin

The Assemblies of God movement became established in Japan during the twentieth century as Pentecostal Christianity developed in the country.


History

The Japan Assemblies of God was established in 1949. Since then, it has developed local churches and Christian ministries while working within Japan's distinctive cultural and religious environment.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, developing churches, training Christian leaders and praying for spiritual renewal and transformation.


Today

WAGF's published profile records 223 churches and 12,146 members for the Japan Assemblies of God. It also notes the challenges created by Japan's aging population and low birth rate and describes the church's desire for spiritual revival and transformation.
