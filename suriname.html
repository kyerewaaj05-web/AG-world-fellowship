Assemblies of God, Suriname

TThe Assemblies of God in Suriname is represented by Stichting Gemeenten Gods Suriname, also identified as Assemblies of God Suriname.


Origin

Assemblies of God ministry developed within Suriname's Pentecostal Christian community.


History

The movement has established congregational and national leadership structures for Pentecostal ministry in the country.


Mission

Its mission is centered on proclaiming the Gospel, developing disciples, strengthening churches and reaching local communities.


Today

Assemblies of God Suriname is represented nationally by its superintendent and participates in the World Assemblies of God Fellowship.
