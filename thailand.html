Assemblies of God, Thailand

Assemblies of God ministry in Thailand is represented by several Pentecostal organizations, including Thailand Assemblies of God, Assemblies of God Fellowship Thailand and Full Gospel Assemblies of Thailand.


Origin

The Assemblies of God movement developed in Thailand through Pentecostal evangelism and the establishment of churches and Christian ministries.


History

The movement has developed multiple church fellowships and ministry structures serving communities in Thailand.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, Christian leadership and ministry to local communities.


Today

WAGF lists multiple Assemblies of God-related organizations in Thailand, reflecting a diverse Pentecostal presence rather than one single national organization.
