Assemblies of God, Romania

The Assemblies of God in Romania form part of the European Pentecostal movement.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Romania through evangelistic activity and the establishment of Pentecostal congregations.


History

Romanian Pentecostal churches expanded through preaching, evangelism, discipleship and the development of local pastoral leadership.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers, strengthen churches and train Christian leaders.


Today

WAGF lists several Romanian organizations, including the Assemblies of God Romania Office, Rugul Aprins Toflea, and BFP Romania / Asociația Religioasa Creștină Neemia Brateiu.
