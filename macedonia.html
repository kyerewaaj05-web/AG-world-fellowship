Assemblies of God, Macedomnia/h1>

The Assemblies of God/Pentecostal movementin Macedonia is part of the European Pentecostal Christian community.


Origin

Pentecostal ministry developed through evangelistic work and the formation of local churches


History

Christian Center Macedonia reports developing churches and pastors with a particular emphasis on missions and establishing new congregations.


Mission

The mission includes evangelism, church planting, discipleship and developing Christian leadership.


Today

WAGF lists Macedonia as a participating location and includes both Christian Center Macedonia and the Evangelical Church in the Republic of Macedonia
