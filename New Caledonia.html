Assemblies of God, New Caledonia

The Assemblies of God in New Caledonia is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving local communities through churches, worship, evangelism and discipleship.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in New Caledonia through the wider Pentecostal movement in the Pacific.


History

Local churches developed and became organized through national Christian leadership and cooperation.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church development and Christian service.


Today

The Union of Assemblies of God in New Caledonia (UADDNC) is listed by WAGF, with leadership based at Pont-des-Français.
