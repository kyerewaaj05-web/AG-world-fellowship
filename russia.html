Assemblies of God,Russia

The Assemblies of God in Russia is part of the wider Pentecostal Christian movement and serves through churches, evangelism, discipleship, worship and Christian ministry.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in the Russian region during the twentieth century, with significant growth occurring through Pentecostal and evangelical movements across the Soviet Union.


History

Pentecostal congregations developed under difficult political and religious circumstances during the Soviet period. After the collapse of the Soviet Union, Pentecostal organizations experienced new opportunities for organized ministry.


Mission

Its mission includes preaching the Gospel, making disciples, establishing churches, training leaders and serving Christian communities.


Today

WAGF lists Russian Assemblies of God as a participating national body, alongside the Russian Church of Christians of Evangelical Faith. This means the Russian Pentecostal landscape should not be presented as one single organization.
