Assemblies Of God,Togo

Assemblies of God in Togo is a Pentecostal Christian movement known nationally as the Église des Assemblées de Dieu du Togo. It is part of the World Assemblies of God Fellowship and has its national leadership based in Lomé. World AG Fellowship


Origin

The movement developed within the Pentecostal missionary expansion of West Africa and gradually established a national church presence.


History

The church has developed through evangelism, local congregations, Christian education and leadership. Its work is also connected with broader regional mission initiatives. A World Assemblies of God Fellowship church-planting document records partnership with Assemblies of God Togo in a major church-planting initiative focussed on young children and people.


Mission

Today, Assemblies of God Ghana continues its ministry through local congregations, evangelism, discipleship, missions, leadership development and community-oriented ministries.


Today

AG Togo continues to develop churches and Christian ministries throughout the country while participating in wider Pentecostal mission efforts.
