Assemblies of God, New Zealand

The Assemblies of God in New Zealand is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through local churches, worship, preaching, discipleship, youth ministry and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in New Zealand during the twentieth century as the Pentecostal revival spread internationally.


History

Assemblies of God churches developed into an organized national movement with churches and ministries throughout the country


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, establish and strengthen churches, and equip believers for Christian service.


Today

The Assemblies of God in New Zealand is listed by WAGF as the national Assemblies of God body, with its listed leadership based in Auckland.
