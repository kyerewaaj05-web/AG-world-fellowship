Assemblies Of God,Cameroon

The Assemblies of God in Cameroon is part of the wider Assemblies of God family of Pentecostal churches. It serves communities across Cameroon through local congregations, Christian teaching, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and ministry to families and communities. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship recognizes Assemblies of God Cameroon as a participating national body.


Origin

The Assemblies of God movement in Cameroon developed within the broader Pentecostal missionary expansion in Central Africa. Over time, the movement became established through national churches and indigenous Christian leadership.


History

The Cameroonian Assemblies of God has developed as a national Pentecostal movement alongside other evangelical and Pentecostal ministries in the country. WAGF records Assemblies of God Cameroon under national leadership in Yaoundé and also lists another Pentecostal body, Full Gospel Mission-Cameroon, separately. This reflects the presence of more than one related Pentecostal organization in Cameroon rather than treating every Pentecostal church as one organization.


Mission

Its mission is centered on proclaiming the Gospel, developing Spirit-empowered disciples, establishing and strengthening churches, and serving communities through Christian ministry.


Today

Today, Assemblies of God Cameroon continues to operate as a national church body with leadership based in Yaoundé. It forms part of the wider international Assemblies of God fellowship while maintaining its own national organization.
