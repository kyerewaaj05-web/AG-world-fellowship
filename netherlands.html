Assemblies of God, Netherlands

The Assemblies of God movement in the Netherlands is represented by Verenigde Pinkster- en Evangeliegemeenten (VPE), a Pentecostal fellowship of Assemblies of God churches.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity reached the Netherlands in 1907, according to the WAGF country profile.


History

The movement developed into a network of Pentecostal churches and ministries. In 1967, the U.S. Assemblies of God helped establish the Dutch Central Bible College. The fellowship subsequently developed ministries involving youth, church planting, missions and theological education.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership training, missions and Christian community.


Today

WAGF's profile describes VPE as a fellowship of 130 Assemblies of God churches, 26,000 members and 200 pastors and preachers. It also reports Dutch churches sending missionaries to several countries.
