Assemblies of God, Norway

The Pentecostal movement in Norway is part of the wider international Pentecostal family. It serves communities through churches, worship, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian ministry.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Norway during the early twentieth century as the Pentecostal revival spread throughout Europe.


History

Norwegian Pentecostal churches developed through local congregations and Christian leaders and gradually formed national structures for cooperation and ministry.


Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, strengthening churches, training Christian leaders and supporting missions.


Today

WAGF lists the Pentecostal Movement in Norway as its participating Pentecostal body, with national leadership based in Oslo.
