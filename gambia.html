Assemblies Of God,Gambia

Assemblies of God in The Gambia is part of the worldwide Pentecostal Assemblies of God family. Its work focuses on sharing the Gospel, developing believers and establishing Christian communities.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in The Gambia emerged through Pentecostal missionary efforts and the development of local Christian ministry.


History

Over time, the work has involved evangelism, local church development and the training and encouragement of believers. Its development reflects the wider Assemblies of God's emphasis on taking the Gospel across cultural and geographical boundaries.


Mission

The church seeks to proclaim Christ, make disciples, strengthen local congregations and equip Christians for service.


Today

AG ministry in The Gambia continues to focus on Christian witness, discipleship and the development of local church communities.
