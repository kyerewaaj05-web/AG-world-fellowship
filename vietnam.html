Assemblies of God,Vietnam

Pentecostal Assemblies of God ministry in Vietnam is part of the wider international Pentecostal movement.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed through evangelistic and church-planting activity in Vietnam.


History

Pentecostal churches have grown through local Christian communities and ministry networks.


Mission

The ministry emphasizes Gospel proclamation, discipleship, church development and Christian service.


Today

Vietnam represents an important Asian Pentecostal context, with Assemblies of God-related ministry connected to the wider international fellowship.
