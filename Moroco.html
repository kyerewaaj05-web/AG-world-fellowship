Assemblies of God, Moroco

Pentecostal Christian ministry in Morocco forms part of the wider international Pentecostal family. Its ministry operates within Morocco's distinctive religious and cultural environment.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Morocco through international Christian contacts and local Christian communities.


History

Pentecostal congregations and Christian ministries have operated in Morocco while navigating the country's religious regulations.


Mission

Its mission centers on Christian worship, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and strengthening believers.


Today

WAGF lists BFP Famille des Assemblées Chrétiennes as a participating Pentecostal body in Morocco, with leadership based in Rabat.
