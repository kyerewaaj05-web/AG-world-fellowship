The Pentecostal Christian movement in Kosovo forms part of the wider European Pentecostal family.


Origin

Pentecostal ministry developed through evangelistic work and the establishment of Christian congregations in Kosovo.


History

Churches have developed through preaching, worship, Bible teaching and evangelism, with local Christian leaders taking responsibility for ministry.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim Jesus Christ, disciple believers and establish Christian communities.


Today

WAGF lists Kosovo separately and identifies the Pentecostal Church of Jesus "Fellowship of the Lord's People" as its participating organization. World AG Fellowship
