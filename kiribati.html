Assemblies of God, Kiribati

The Assemblies of God of Kiribati is the national Pentecostal fellowship in Kiribati affiliated with the World Assemblies of God Fellowship, serving churches across the Gilbert Islands, Line Islands, and Phoenix Islands.


Origin

Pentecostal ministry developed through missionary activity and local Christian leadership.


History

Churches have grown through evangelism, worship, prayer and discipleship. Ministry has included local church development, youth and family ministry and leadership training.


Mission

The mission is to proclaim the Gospel, develop disciples and strengthen Christian communities across the islands.


Today

WAGF's published profile reports Kiribati forms part of the Pacific/Oceania field of Assemblies of God ministry. The national fellowship is headquartered in Eita, Tarawa.
