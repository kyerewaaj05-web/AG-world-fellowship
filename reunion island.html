Assemblies of God, Reunion

The Assemblies of God presence in Reunion belongs to the Pentecostal Christian movement of the Indian Ocean.


Origin

Pentecostal ministry developed on the island through evangelism, missionary activity and the establishment of local Christian congregations.


History

The movement developed through preaching, worship, discipleship and community ministry, with French-speaking Christian ministry playing an important role.


Mission

The mission is to proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers and strengthen churches on Reunion while participating in wider Indian Ocean and international missions.


Today

Reunion should be retained as its own location, rather than being merged into mainland France or another African country. Its geographical classification is Indian Ocean / Africa region.
