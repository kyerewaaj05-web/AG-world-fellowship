Assemblies of God, Dominican Republic

The Assemblies of God in the Dominican Republic is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through local churches, evangelism, discipleship, worship, Christian education and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity spread through Caribbean and Latin America during the twentieth century. The Assemblies of Go movement subsequently developed a national presence in the Dominican Republic.


History

Local congregations developed into an organized national fellowship. The movement has participated in evangelisn and church development while building national Christian leadership.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, mal disciples, establish and strengthen churches, train ministers and serve communities through Christian ministry.


Today

The Concilio Evangélico Nacional de las Asambleas de Dios en la República Dominicana is the national Assemblies of God body listed by WAGF, with its leadership based in Santo Domingo. Historical WAGF material also identifies the Dominican Republic among the Latin American countries where Pentecostal Christianity became strongly established.
