Assemblies of God, Moldova

TThe Pentecostal movement in Moldova is part of the wider European and Eurasian Pentecostal family. It serves through churches, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian teaching.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Moldova during the twentieth century, with further organizational development after the Soviet period.


History

Pentecostal churches developed national structures and relationships with churches in neighboring countries.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, planting churches, training leaders and serving communities.


Today

The Pentecostal Union of the Republic of Moldova is listed by WAGF, with leadership based in Chișinău.
