Assemblies of God,Crotia

The Evangelical Pentecostal Church in Croatia belongs to the wider evangelical and Pentecostal Christian family. Its identity combines an evangelical emphasis on the authority of Scripture with a Pentecostal emphasis on the work of the Holy Spirit in Christian life and community.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Croatia during the twentieth century alongside the wider Pentecostal movement in Europe. The Evangelical Pentecostal Church emerged from the development and cooperation of evangelical and Pentecostal Christian movements.


History

Over time, Assemblies of God congregations developed into an organized national fellowship known as Las Asambleas de Dios de Chile. The movement has continued to develop churches and Christian ministries throughout the country.


Mission

Its mission is centered on biblical teaching, Christian worship, evangelism, discipleship and living out the Christian faith through the work of the Holy Spirit.


Today

The Evangelical Pentecostal Church in Croatia remains an independent Christian community connected to the wider evangelical and Pentecostal family. WAGF includes Croatia among its participating Pentecostal countries.
