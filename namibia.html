Assemblies Of God,Namibia

The General Council of the Assemblies of God of Namibia is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities across Namibia. It forms part of the wider international Assemblies of God fellowship.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Namibia developed within the wider Pentecostal movement in Southern Africa. The work grew through evangelism, Christian fellowship and the establishment of local congregations.


History

As the movement developed, local churches and Namibian Christian leaders became increasingly important in carrying out ministry. A national Assemblies of God structure developed to support churches and coordinate the work.


Mission

The church seeks to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, establish churches, train leaders and serve communities.


Today

AG Namibia continues its ministry through local churches and Christian workers. Its national council is based in Windhoek.
