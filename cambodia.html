Assemblies of God,Cambodia

The Assemblies of God of Cambodia is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities throughout Cambodia through local churches, evangelism, discipleship, children's ministry, leadership development and church planting.


Origin

The movement developed particularly around Phnom Penh, where Assemblies of God missionaries were based and where ministry projects helped establish an early foundation for church growth.


History

Church growth subsequently spread beyond Phnom Penh into areas including Takeo, Siem Reap, Sihanoukville and Battambang. WAGF's profile records churches, developing churches and recent church plants, together with a Bible institute and Cambodian pastoral leadership.


Mission

The church emphasizes evangelism, discipleship, church planting and training national Christian leaders. Education and practical community ministry have also formed part of its work.


Today

The Assemblies of God of Cambodia has expanded into numerous provinces. WAGF's published profile records 51 churches, 65 developing churches and 32 recent plants, with Assemblies of God work present in 15 of Cambodia's 24 provinces.
