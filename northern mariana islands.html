Assemblies of God, Northern Mariana Islands

The General Council of the Assemblies of God of the Northern Mariana and Palau Islands is the regional fellowship serving both the Northern Mariana Islands and Palau, affiliated with the World Assemblies of God Fellowship.


Origin

Pentecostal ministry developed through missionary activity and the establishment of local congregations.


History

Churches developed through evangelism, worship, Bible teaching and discipleship. Since then, ministries have included family, youth, children's and community outreach.


Mission

The mission is to proclaim the Gospel and strengthen Christian communities throughout the islands.


Today

The Northern Mariana Islands continue to serve society, win souls for Christ and nurture believers even though it is not a sovereign state. The regional fellowship is headquartered in Saipan and oversees churches in both the Northern Mariana Islands and Palau.
