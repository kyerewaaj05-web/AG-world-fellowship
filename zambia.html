Assemblies Of God,Zambia

Assemblies of God in Zambia is a Pentecostal Christian movement dedicated to worship, evangelism, discipleship and Christian service. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship recognizes Assemblies of God in Zambia as well as another Pentecostal Assemblies of God body in the country.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Zambia developed as Pentecostal Christianity expanded across Southern Africa. Missionary activity and the response of local Christians contributed to the establishment and development of Assemblies of God congregations.


History

The church grew through evangelistic ministry, local congregations and the development of indigenous Christian leadership. Over time, Assemblies of God churches became established in different communities, creating a national network of Christian ministry.


Mission

The church focuses on evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development and missions.


Today

AG Zambia continues to serve through churches and Christian workers, seeking to strengthen believers and extend the Gospel to additional communities. The national Assemblies of God leadership is based in Lusaka.
