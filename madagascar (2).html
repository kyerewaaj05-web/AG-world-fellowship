Assemblies Of God,Madagascar

The Assemblies of God of Madagascar is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities across Madagascar. It is part of the worldwide Assemblies of God fellowship and has developed an extensive network of churches and Christian workers.


Origin

The movement developed through Pentecostal missionary activity and the growth of local Christian communities in Madagascar. As the work expanded, Madagascar believers and ministers became increasingly important in the development of the national church.


History

The Assemblies of God of Madagascar has experienced substantial growth. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship describes a national movement with approximately 800 churches, 350 pastors and 100,000 adherents in its published country profile.


Mission

The church focuses on evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development and Christian service.


Today

AG Madagascar continues to serve through its large network of churches and pastors, seeking to proclaim Christ and strengthen Christian communities throughout the country.
