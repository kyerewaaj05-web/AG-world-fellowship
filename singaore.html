Assemblies of God,Singapore

The Assemblies of God of Singapore is the national Assemblies of God fellowship serving Pentecostal churches and ministries in Singapore.


Origin

The Assemblies of God movement in Singapore developed as part of the wider Pentecostal movement and became organized through national church structures.


History

Over time, Assemblies of God churh in Singapore have developed congregational, pastoral, evangelistic and leadership ministries.


Mission

Its mission centers on proclaiming the Gospel, discipling believers, developing Christian leaders and strengthening local churches.


Today

The Assemblies of God of Singapore is represented nationally within the World Assemblies of God Fellowship.
