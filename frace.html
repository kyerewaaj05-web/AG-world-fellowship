Assemblies of God,France

The Assemblies of God in France, known as Assemblées de Dieu de France, is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through local churches, evangelism, discipleship, worship, Christian education and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity began developing in France during the twentieth century as the Pentecostal revival spread through Europe. Missionary activity and French Pentecostal leaders contributed to the establishment of local congregations.


History

Over time, Pentecostal churches developed into organized national networks. The Assemblies of God became one of the Pentecostal movements serving communities throughout France.


Mission

Its mission focuses on proclaiming the Gospel, forming disciples, planting and strengthening churches, training leaders and participating in missionary work.


Today

Assemblées de Dieu de France is the national Assemblies of God body listed by WAGF for France, with its listed national leadership based in Salles-d'Aude.
