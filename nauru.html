Assemblies of God, Nauru

TThe Assemblies of God of Nauru is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving the island community through worship, evangelism, discipleship and Christian fellowship.


Origin

Assemblies of God ministry developed in Nauru as Pentecostal Christianity spread through the Pacific.


History

Local Christian ministry developed into an organized Assemblies of God presence serving the island community.


Mission

Its mission includes preaching the Gospel, discipling believers, strengthening families and developing Christian ministry.


Today

The Assemblies of God of Nauru is listed by WAGF, with its national leadership based in Nauru.
