Assemblies Of God,Liberia

Assemblies of God in Liberia is part of the worldwide Pentecostal Assemblies of God fellowship. Its ministry is centred on proclaiming Jesus Christ, strengthening Christian believers and developing local churches.


Origin

The AG presence in Liberia developed through Pentecostal missionary work and the establishment of local Christian communities.


History

The movement has developed alongside the broader growth of Pentecostal Christianity in Liberia, with local churches and Christian leaders carrying forward the work.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, develop Christian leaders and establish churches capable of serving their communities.


Today

AG Liberia continues its Christian witness through local congregations, evangelism, discipleship and ministry.
