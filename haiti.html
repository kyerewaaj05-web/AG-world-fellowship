Assemblies of God,Haiti

The Assemblies of God in Haiti is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving Haitian communities through churches, evangelism, discipleship, worship, Christian education and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Haiti during the twentieth century as Pentecostal missionaries and Haitian Christian leaders established churches and ministries.


History

The movement developed a national church presence through evangelism, church planting and the training of Haitian pastors and Christian workers. Local churches became important centers for worship, teaching and community ministry.


Mission

Its mission focuses on sharing the Gospel, making disciples, developing churches, training Christian leaders and serving Haitian communities.


Today

Les Assemblées de Dieu en Haïti is listed by WAGF as the Assemblies of God national body in Haiti, with its listed leadership based in Pétion-Ville.
