Assemblies of God,Serbia

The Assemblies of God presence in Serbia is represented through the Christ Evangelical Church (Hristova Jevandjeoska Crkva), a Pentecostal church serving people from several ethnic backgrounds.


Origin

Its work developed within Serbia's Pentecostal and evangelical Christian community.


History

The church has developed ministries focused on children, young people, evangelism, leadership development and cooperation with other Pentecostal and evangelical churches. A Pentecostal Bible school was established in 2011.


Mission

Its stated goals include reaching the next generation, building bridges within society, raising new leaders, and planting churches.


Today

The movement serves Serbian, Hungarian, Slovakian and Roma communities and organizes camps, clubs and youth conferences.
