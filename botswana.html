Assemblies Of God,Botswana

Assemblies of God in Botswana is a Pentecostal Christian movement committed to worshipping God, proclaiming the Gospel and building Christian communities throughout the country.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Botswana developed through Pentecostal missionary activity in Southern Africa and the establishment of local Christian congregations.


History

As the work developed, local leadership and congregations became increasingly important to the growth of the movement. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship recognizes the Assemblies of God in Botswana as the national AG body.


Mission

The church focuses on evangelism, discipleship, church development, Christian leadership and service.


Today

AG Botswana continues its ministry through local churches and Christian workers, seeking to reach communities and strengthen believers in their faith.
