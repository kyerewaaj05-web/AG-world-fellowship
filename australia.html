Assemblies of God,Australia

Assemblies of God ministry in Australia is represented within the Australian Christian Churches, a Pentecostal movement connected with the worldwide Assemblies of God family. Its churches serve communities through worship, preaching, discipleship, children's and youth ministry, leadership development and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Australia during the twentieth century through revival movements, independent Pentecostal congregations and missionary influences. These developments eventually contributed to organized Pentecostal fellowships.


History

Over time, Pentecostal churches across Australia developed stronger cooperation and national structures. The Australian Christian Churches is the national body listed by WAGF for Australia's Assemblies of God fellowship.


Mission

The movement seeks to introduce people to Jesus Christ, build healthy churches, disciple believers, train leaders and participate in local and international missions.


Today

Australian Christian Churches continues to serve as a major Pentecostal fellowship in Australia and maintains its connection with the worldwide Assemblies of God movement.
