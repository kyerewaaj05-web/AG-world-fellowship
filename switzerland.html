Assemblies of God, Switzerland

The Pentecostal Assemblies of Switzerland, known as Schweizerische Pfingstmission (SPM), represents a significant Assemblies of God Pentecostal presence in Switzerland.


Origin

SPM was founded in 1936 as part of the development of Pentecostal Christianity in Switzerland.


History

The movement has grown through local churches, evangelism, church planting and missionary activity. Its ministry has developed within Switzerland's multilingual and culturally diverse environment.


Mission

SPM emphasizes Spirit-filled pastors, Spirit-led church life, Spirit-empowered evangelism and missions.


Today

WAGF reports 67 churches and about 10,000 church members. SPM is also active in missions across several continents and in more than 50 countries.
