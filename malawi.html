Assemblies Of God, Malawi

Assemblies of God in Malawi is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities throughout Malawi. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship recognizes both Assemblies of God in Malawi and Pentecostal Assemblies of God in Malawi.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Malawi developed as part of the wider Pentecostal missionary movement in Southern Africa. Local believers and Christian leaders subsequently became increasingly involved in the development of the church.


History

The movement developed through evangelism, church planting and the establishment of local congregations. As the church grew, national Christian leadership and ministry structures developed to support the work.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming Christ, making disciples, establishing churches, training leaders and serving communities.


Today

AG Malawi continues its ministry through local congregations and Christian workers, seeking to strengthen the church and reach people with the Gospel.
