Assemblies of God, Montenegro

Pentecostal Christians in Montenegro form part of the wider international Pentecostal movement.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Montenegro within the wider spread of Pentecostalism across the Balkans.


History

Local Pentecostal Christian communities developed through evangelism and the work of Christian leaders.


Mission

The movement seeks to proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers, develop churches and strengthen Christian fellowship.


Today

WAGF lists Lighthouse Christian Center as a participating Pentecostal body in Montenegro.
