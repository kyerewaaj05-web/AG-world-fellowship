Assemblies of God, Finland

The entecostal movement in Finland is part of the wider international Pentecostal family represented within the World Assemblies of God Fellowship. It serves communities through local churches, worship, evangelism, discipleship, Christian teaching and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Finland during the early twentieth century as the Pentecostal revival spread across Europe.


History

The movement grew through local congregations and Christian workers and eventually developed organized national structures. Finnish Pentecostal churches have maintained a strong emphasis on preaching, prayer, missions and Christian community.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, strengthening churches, training Christian workers and supporting missionary work.


Today

WAGF's profile of the Pentecostal Church of Finland records approximately 240 churches and 400 pastors, with roughly 65,000 people, including children and youth, connected with its churches.
