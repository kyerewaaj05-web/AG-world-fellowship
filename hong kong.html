Assemblies of God,Hong Kong

Assemblies of God churches and Pentecostal organizations in Hong Kong serve a major international and Chinese-speaking urban community.


Origin

Pentecostal ministry developed in Hong Kong through missionary activity and the establishment of indigenous Christian congregations.


History

Churches developed ministries in evangelism, Bible teaching, worship, discipleship and community service.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church development and reaching people in Hong Kong and beyond.


Today

WAGF currently lists two Hong Kong organizations in its participating-country directory, including the Asia Assembly Mission Council and the Assemblies of God Hong Kong Association.
