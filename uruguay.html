Assemblies of God, Uruguay

The Assemblies of God in Uruguay is represented by the Concilio General de las Asambleas de Dios en el Uruguay.


Origin

Assemblies of God ministry developed in Uruguay through Pentecostal evangelism and the establishment of local churches.


History

The movement has developed a national network of churches, pastors, believers and missionary work.


Mission

Its mission includes preaching the Gospel, making disciples, strengthening churches and sending missionaries.


Today

WAGF reports 154 churches, approaching 18,000 adherents, 238 pastors and 11 missionaries fully supported by the Uruguayan church.
