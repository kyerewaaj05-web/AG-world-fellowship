Assemblies of God,Tonga

The Tongan Assemblies of God Churches represent the Assemblies of God Pentecostal movement in Tonga..


Origin

The movement developed through Pentecostal Christian ministry and the establishment of Assemblies of God churches in Tonga.


History

The churches have developed local congregational ministry and national leadership structures.


Mission

Their mission is to proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers, strengthen families and communities, and establish churches.


Today

The Tongan Assemblies of God Churches are represented within the World Assemblie of God Fellowship.
