Assemblies of God, West Indies

The Pentecostal Assemblies of the West Indies (PAWI) is the regional fellowship serving Trinidad & Tobago and the wider Caribbean, affiliated with the World Assemblies of God Fellowship. It oversees hundreds of churches and ministers across the region.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity spread throughout the Caribbean through missionaries, evangelists and indigenous Christian leaders.


History

Assemblies of God and related Pentecostal churches developed across numerous islands and territories, producing regional networks of churches and ministries.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers and strengthen Pentecostal churches throughout the Caribbean.


Today

The movement has emphasized evangelism, missions, pastoral training, church planting and ministry to Caribbean communities. WAGF lists West Indies as a participating location and identifies the Pentecostal Assemblies of the West Indies as its organization, headquartered in Port of Spain, Trinidad.
