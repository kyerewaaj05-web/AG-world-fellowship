Assemblies of God,Bolivia

The Assemblies of God in Bolivia are part of the large Pentecostal and evangelical Christian movement in South America.


Origin

The Assemblies of God in Bolivia are part of the large Pentecostal and evangelical Christian movement in South America.


History

Bolivian Assemblies of God churches expanded through evangelism, church planting and discipleship, reaching communities across different geographical and cultural settings.


Mission

The mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, plant churches and equip Christian leaders.


Today

Bolivia forms part of the Latin American Assemblies of God family and contributes to the wider Pentecostal mission of the region.
