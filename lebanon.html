Assemblies of God,Lebanon

The Assemblies of God-related Pentecostal movement in Lebanon is part of the wider international Pentecostal family. It serves Christian communities through worship, biblical teaching, evangelism, discipleship and prayer.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Lebanon during the twentieth century as the Pentecostal movement expanded throughout the Middle East.


History

Pentecostal churches and ministries developed within Lebanon's diverse Christian environment, with local congregations becoming centers of worship, teaching and evangelism.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, strengthen churches and equip believers for Christian service.


Today

Pentecostal ministry continues in Lebanon through churches and Christian ministries connected with the wider international Pentecostal movement.
