Assemblies of God,Portugal

The Assemblies of God in Portugal is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through local churches, worship, evangelism, discipleship and Christian education.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Portugal during the twentieth century as the movement spread throughout Europe.


History

Local Pentecostal congregations developed into organized national structures, with Portuguese Christian leaders taking increasing responsibility for church ministry.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, establishing churches and training Christian workers.


Today

The Convenção das Assembleias de Deus em Portugal is listed by WAGF as the national Assemblies of God body, with leadership based in Lisbon.
