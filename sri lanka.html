Assemblies of God, Sri Lanka

The Assemblies of God presence in Sri Lanka is represented by the Assemblies of God of Ceylon.


Origin

The movement developed within Sri Lanka's Pentecostal Christian community and became organized through national Assemblies of God ministry.


History

Assemblies of God churches have established congregations and Christian ministry in Sri Lanka as part of the wider Pentecostal movement.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, establish and strengthen churches, and equip believers for Christian service.


Today

The Assemblies of God of Ceylon is represented within the World Assemblies of God Fellowship. WAGF also lists another Pentecostal fellowship connected with Germany in its Sri Lanka entry, so the country's listing includes more than one related body.
