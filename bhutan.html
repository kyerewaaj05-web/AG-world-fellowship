Assemblies of God,Bhutan

The Assemblies of God presence associated with Bhutan forms part of the wider Pentecostal movement serving South and Central Asian communities.


Origin

Pentecostal ministry in the region developed through missionary activity, Christian workers and indigenous believers establishing local fellowships.


History

Christian ministry in Bhutan has operated within a distinctive religious and cultural environment. Pentecostal work has consequently emphasized personal faith, discipleship, prayer and small Christian fellowships.


Mission

The mission is to proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers and develop healthy Christian communities.


Today

Bhutan appears in the WAGF participating-country directory, although its listing is associated with the wider Pentecostal fellowship structure rather than a large independent national Assemblies of God council.
