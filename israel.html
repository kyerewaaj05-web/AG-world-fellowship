Assemblies of God,Israel

The Pentecostal Assemblies of God-related movement in Israel serves Christian communities through worship, biblical teaching, prayer, evangelism, discipleship and fellowship. WAGF lists BFP Israel UIIC as its participating Pentecostal body for Israel.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in the region during the twentieth century as the worldwide Pentecostal movement expanded and Christian communities adopted Pentecostal teaching and ministry.


History

Pentecostal churches and ministries developed among different Christian communities in Israel. The movement has maintained connections with international Pentecostal organizations while developing ministry within its local context.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, developing disciples, strengthening Christian fellowship, training leaders and supporting Christian ministry.


Today

BFP Israel UIIC is listed by WAGF with its leadership based in Kfar-Saba. Because Israel contains several Christian traditions and organizations, this page should specifically identify the Assemblies of God/ Pentecostal body rather than describing all Christians in Israel as one organization.
