Assemblies of God, Ecuador

The Assemblies of God in Ecuador is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through churches, worship, evangelism, discipleship, Christian education and missions.


Origin

The movement developed during the expansion of Pentecostal Christianity in Latin America during the twentieth century. Missionary work and indigenous Christian leadership contributed to the establishment and growth of Pentecostal congregations.


History

As churches were established throughout Ecuador, the movement developed national leadership and organized structures for church ministry, evangelism and discipleship.


Mission

Its mission focuses on preaching the Gospel, making disciples, planting and strengthening churches, training Christian leaders and extending Christian ministry to communities.


Today

WAGF lists Asambleas de Dios as the Assemblies of God national body for Ecuador, with its listed leadership based in Quito.
