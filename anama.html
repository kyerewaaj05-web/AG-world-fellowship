Assemblies of God,Panama

The Assemblies of God in Panama is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through worship, evangelism, discipleship, Christian education and missions.


Origin

The movement developed during the expansion of Pentecostal Christianity through Central America and Latin America.


History

Assemblies of God congregations developed into a national church movement with organized leadership and ministries throughout Panama.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, plant and strengthen churches and train Christian leaders.


Today

The Concilio General de las Asambleas de Dios en Panamá is listed by WAGF as the national Assemblies of God body for Panama, with leadership based in Panama City.
