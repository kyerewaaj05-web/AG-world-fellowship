Assemblies Of God,Nigeria

The Assemblies of God, Nigeria is a major Pentecostal Christian church movement serving communities throughout Nigeria. It is part of the worldwide Assemblies of God fellowship and carries out ministry through local churches, evangelism, discipleship, ministerial training and missions.


Origin

The Nigerian Assemblies of God emerged from the wider Pentecostal missionary movement and developed into an indigenous national church with its own leadership, churches and ministry structures.

Mission

Its mission includes preaching the Gospel, making disciples, planting and strengthening churches, training ministers and sending missionaries beyond Nigeria.


Today

WAGF currently describes Assemblies of God Nigeria as having approximately 2.6 million adherents and members worshipping in more than 14,300 churches. It also reports more than 11,650 credentialed ministers and more than 50 missionaries serving in over nine countries and territories.
