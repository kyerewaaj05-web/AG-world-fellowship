Assemblies of God,Costa Rica

The Assemblies of God in Costa Rica is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving the country through local congregations, worship, evangelism, discipleship, Christian education and missions.


Origin

The movement developed as Pentecostal Christianity expanded through Central America during the twentieth century. Local believers and ministers helped establish congregations and develop national Christian leadership.


History

The Assemblies of God became organized as a national movement through churches and ministry structures established across Costa Rica. Its work has included evangelism, church development and the training of Christian workers.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, strengthen churches, train leaders and serve people through Christian ministry.


Today

Asambleas de Dios en Costa Rica is the national Assemblies of God body listed by WAGF for Costa Rica. Its listed national leadership is based in San José.
