Assemblies Of God,Egypt

The Assemblies of God in Egypt is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through local churches, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian education. It is represented within the worldwide Assemblies of God fellowship.


Origin

The Assemblies of God of Egypt developed during the early twentieth century as Pentecostal Christianity took root in Egypt. WAGF describes the Egyptian Assemblies of God as having been formed in the early twentieth century.


History

Over the decades, the movement established churches and developed approaches suited to the Egyptian context. WAGF reports that by 2014 the Assemblies of God of Egypt had approximately 145 churches. Since then, its church-planting strategy has increasingly emphasized home-based small groups as a way of establishing Christian communities in areas without churches.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting and developing Christian leaders. A particular emphasis has been placed on beginning with small groups and developing those groups into local congregations.


Today

The movement continues to pursue church planting through small-group ministry and leadership training. WAGF reports that the strategy includes identifying areas without churches, training prospective ministers and sending them to begin home-based groups.
