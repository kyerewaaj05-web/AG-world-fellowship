Assemblies Of God,United Kingdom - Great Britain

Assemblies of God Great Britain is a Pentecostal Christian denomination serving communities throughout Great Britain. Its churches vary in size and style but share a commitment to Christian worship, teaching, discipleship, evangelism and ministry.


Origin

Assemblies of God has been serving people in Great Britain since 1924, according to its WAGF profile.


History

Since its establishment, the movement has developed a network of local churches and ministries. Churches have expanded their work beyond Sunday worship to include children's, youth and young-adult ministries, teaching, training and community ministry.


Mission

Its stated purpose is to give people the opportunity to understand the Gospel and provide churches where believers can grow and serve in ministry.


Today

WAGF reports that Assemblies of God Great Britain has a network of more than 500 local churches. These churches serve their communities through a range of ministries while remaining part of the national movement.
