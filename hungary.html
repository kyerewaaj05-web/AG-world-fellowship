Assemblies of God,Hungary

The Pentecostal movement represented within the Assemblies of God family in Hungary is part of the wider international Pentecostal community. It serves through churches, worship, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian teaching.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Hungary during the twentieth century as the Pentecostal revival spread across Europe.


History

Hungarian Pentecostal churches developed through local congregations and Christian leaders. The movement eventually developed organized national structures and relationships with Pentecostal churches internationally.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, forming disciples, developing churches, training Christian leaders and participating in missions.


Today

WAGF lists the Evangelical Pentecostal Fellowship as the participating Pentecostal body for Hungary, with its listed leadership based in Budapest.
