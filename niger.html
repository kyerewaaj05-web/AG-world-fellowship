Assemblies of God, Niger

The Assemblies of God of Niger is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through churches, evangelism, discipleship, Christian education and community ministry.


Origin

According to the WAGF country profile, Assemblies of God work in Niger began in 1990, with missionaries from France, Burkina Faso and the United States. Other missionaries later joined from several West African countries.


History

A national church was formed in 2006 and came under national leadership. WAGF records the development of churches, pastors and specialized ministries for children, women and youth.


Mission

The movement emphasizes evangelism and church planting. Its ministry also includes practical community development through Kamna, an organization involved in education, health and water-related assistance.


Today

WAGF's published profile records 60 churches, 50 pastors and approximately 4,000 members. Its stated vision includes establishing churches in towns and villages while addressing serious community needs.
