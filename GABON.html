Assemblies Of God,Gabon

Assemblies of God Gabon is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities in Gabon. It forms part of the worldwide Assemblies of God fellowship and is represented nationally by Assemblies of God Gabon.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Gabon developed as Pentecostal Christianity spread through Central Africa and missionary work established new Christian communities.


History

The movement developed through local congregations and Christian leadership, becoming part of the broader Assemblies of God network in Central Africa. Gabon has also been connected with regional theological and pastoral training involving Assemblies of God workers from neighbouring countries.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church development, leadership training and Christian service.


Today

AG Gabon continues to operate through local churches and Christian ministry, seeking to strengthen believers and reach additional communities with the Gospel.
