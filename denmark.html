Assemblies of God, Denmark

The Assemblies of God-related Pentecostal movement in Denmark is represented by the Danish Pentecostal Movement, known as MOSAIK. It forms part of the wider Pentecostal family and maintains international relationships within the World Assemblies of God Fellowship.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity reached Denmark during the early twentieth century as the Pentecostal revival spread across Europe.


History

Danish Pentecostal churches developed into a national movement with congregations working together in fellowship, ministry and missions. The movement has also maintained relationships with Pentecostal churches in all countries


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, developing disciples, planting and strengthening churches, training Christian leaders and participating in missions.


Today

WAGF lists MOSAIK - Danish Pentecostal Movement as the participating Pentecostal body for Denmark, with its listed leadership based in Mariager.
