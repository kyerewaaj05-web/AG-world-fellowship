Assemblies of God,Greece

The Assemblies of God-related Pentecostal movement in Greece is part of the wider Pentecostal Christian family. It includes churches and house churches serving communities throughout the country.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Greece during the twentieth century as the Pentecostal movement spread through Europe and the Mediterranean region.


History

Pentecostal congregations developed in cities and villages across Greece. Over time, different Pentecostal church structures emerged, including the Greek Synod of Apostolic Pentecostal Church and other congregations.


Mission

The movement's ministry includes evangelism, biblical teaching, discipleship, prayer, church development, radio ministry, missions and ministry among refugees.


Today

WAGF's published profile reports approximately 200 churches and a similar number of house churches, with about 90 pastors. It notes that these churches include congregations belonging to the Greek Synod, Foursquare Church, Church of Prophecy and independent Pentecostal churches.
