Assemblies of God,Albania

The Assemblies of God in Albania is part of the worldwide Pentecostal Assemblies of God family. It serves Albanian communities through local churches, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian ministry. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship lists the General Council of the Assemblies of God of Albania as a participating national body.


Origin

The movement developed within the wider growth of Pentecostal Christianity in Albania and the Balkans, eventually developing an organized national Assemblies of God structure.


History

As the movement developed, local Christian leadership and congregations became central to its continuing work. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship recognizes the Sierra Leone Assemblies of God as a national participating body, with its leadership based in Freetown.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, establish and strengthen churches, and equip believers for Christian service.


Today

The Assemblies of God in Albania continues its ministry as part of the international Assemblies of God fellowship, with its national leadership based in Durrës.
