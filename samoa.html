Assemblies of God,Samoa

me Assemblies of God in Samoa is represented nationally by the General Council of the Assemblies of God in Samoa. It forms part of the wider World Assemblies of God Fellowship.


Origin

The movement developed within Samoa's Pentecostal Christian community and became organized through a national Assemblies of God structure.


History

The Samoan Assemblies of God has developed churches and Christian ministries throughout the country, with national leadership based in Apia.


Mission

Its mission is centered on proclaiming the Gospel, strengthening believers, developing Christian leaders, and establishing churches.


Today

Today, the General Council of the Assemblies of God in Samoa is led nationally by its General Superintendent and participates in the international Assemblies of God fellowship.
