Assemblies of God,Jamaica

The Assemblies of God in Jamaica is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through local churches, worship, evangelism, discipleship, Christian education and missions.


Origin

The movement developed as Pentecostal Christianity spread through the Caribbean during the twentieth century. Local believers and ministers helped establish churches and develop a national Pentecostal presence.


History

Assemblies of God congregations grew through evangelism, Christian teaching and the development of national leadership. Churches became centers for worship, discipleship and community ministry.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, plant and strengthen churches, train Christian leaders and serve Jamaican communities.


Today

The General Council of the Assemblies of God in Jamaica Inc. is listed by WAGF as the national Assemblies of God body for Jamaica, with its listed leadership based in Kingston.
