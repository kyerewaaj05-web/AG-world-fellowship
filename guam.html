Assemblies of God,Guam

The Assemblies of God in Guam form part of the Pentecostal Christian community of Micronesia and the wider Pacific.


Origin

Pentecostal ministry developed through missionary work and the establishment of local congregations.


History

Churches developed through evangelism, worship, discipleship and community ministry and has supported congregational development, youth ministry, Bible teaching and missions.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Christ, disciple believers and serve communities in Guam and the wider Pacific.


Today

Today, the Guam Assemblies of God continues its ministry and remains connected to the wider World Assemblies of God Fellowship.
