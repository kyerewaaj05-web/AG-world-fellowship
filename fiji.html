Assemblies of God, Fiji

The Assemblies of God of Fiji is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities across the Fiji Islands through churches, worship, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian ministry.


Origin

The Assemblies of God movement developed in Fiji as Pentecostal Christianity expanded through the Pacific region and local Christian communities became organized into national churches.


History

Over time, Assemblies of God churches developed national leadership and ministry structures. The movement has participated in evangelism, church development and the training of Christian workers.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, strengthen local churches, train disciples. Christian leaders and extend Christian ministry throughout Fiji and beyond.


Today

The Assemblies of God of Fiji is listed by WAGF as the national Assemblies of God body for Fiji, with its listed leadership based in Suva.
