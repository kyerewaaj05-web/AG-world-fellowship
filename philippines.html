Assemblies Of God,Philippines

The Philippines Assemblies of God is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities throughout the Philippines through churches, evangelism, discipleship, Christian education and missions.


Origin

The Philippines Assemblies of God was formally organized in 1953.


History

Since its organization, the movement has expanded through church planting and ministry development across the country's islands.


Mission

Its mission includes preaching the Gospel, making disciples, planting churches, training Christian leaders and sending missionaries.


Today

WAGF's published profile describes the Philippines Assemblies of God as having approximately 4,000 local churches, making it a substantial Pentecostal church network in the country.
