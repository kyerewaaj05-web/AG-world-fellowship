Assemblies of God, Pakistan

The Assemblies of God in Pakistan is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through churches, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian teaching.


Origin

Assemblies of God ministry developed in Pakistan within the wider expansion of Pentecostal Christianity in South Asia.


History

Local congregations and Christian leaders developed Pentecostal ministry in different parts of the country, eventually establishing organized national leadership.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, establishing churches, training Christian workers and serving communities.


Today

The Pakistan Assemblies of God is listed by WAGF as the national Assemblies of God body, with leadership based in Lahore.
