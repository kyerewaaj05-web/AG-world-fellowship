Assemblies Of God,Cape Verde

Assemblies of God in Cape Verde is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities across the island nation. The church combines evangelism, discipleship, Christian education and practical community service, seeking to communicate the Gospel while responding to the needs of people in its communities.


Origin

The Assemblies of God work in Cape Verde developed as part of the wider Pentecostal missionary movement, with the church eventually establishing congregations in several of the country's islands and major communities.


History

The movement has developed churches particularly in Praia, Assomada and São Vicente. Alongside church ministry, the Assemblies of God in Cape Verde has developed practical outreach programmes, including neighbourhood cell groups, feeding initiatives and assistance to people experiencing poverty. It also has Bible training and ministries serving children and women.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming Jesus Christ, developing disciples, training Christian leaders and demonstrating Christian love through practical service to communities.


Today

The Assemblies of God in Cape Verde continues to combine church ministry with community outreach, Christian education and practical assistance, seeking to strengthen local congregations and reach people with the Gospel.
