Assemblies of God,Austria

The Assemblies of God/Pentecostal movement in Austria forms part of Europe's wider Pentecostal Christian community. It is represented within the worldwide Assemblies of God fellowship.


Origin

Pentecostal ministry in Austria developed during the expansion of Pentecostal Christianity across Europe, with churches being established through evangelistic work and relationships among Pentecostal believers.


The Austrian movement developed through local congregations, ministry organizations and cooperation with other Pentecostal bodies. Its work has included evangelism, worship, Bible teaching and discipleship.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers and strengthen Christian witness within Austrian communities.


Today

Austria belongs to the European portion of the wider Assemblies of God family and contributes to international Pentecostal fellowship.
