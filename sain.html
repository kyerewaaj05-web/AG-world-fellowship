Assemblies of God, Spain

The Assemblies of God in Spain is represented by the Federación Asambleas de Dios de España (FADE), serving churches and ministers throughout the country.


Origin

Assemblies of God ministry in Spain developed through Pentecostal evangelism and the establishment of local churches.


History

The Spanish movement has expanded through church planting, Bible education, evangelism and missionary activity. Its Bible school and extension schools have contributed to ministerial formation.


Mission

The national church identifies four major areas of ministry: evangelism, church planting, missions and ministerial formation.


Today

WAGF reports 225 churches, about 80 and approximately 400 credentialed ministers. Spain also sends missionaries to other countries.
