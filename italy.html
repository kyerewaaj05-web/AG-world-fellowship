Assemblies of God,Italy

The Assemblies of God in Italy, known as Assemblee di Dio in Italia, is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through churches, worship, evangelism, discipleship, biblical teaching and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Italy during the early twentieth century as the Pentecostal revival spread through Europe. Italian believers and Christian workers contributed to the establishment of Pentecostal congregations.


History

The movement developed through local churches and national Christian leadership. Over time, the Assemblee di Dio in Italia became an organized national Pentecostal body serving congregations throughout the country.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, strengthening churches, training Christian workers and supporting missionary activity.


Today

Assemblee di Dio in Italia is listed by WAGF as the national Assemblies of God body for Italy, with its listed leadership based in Fonte Nuova near Rome.
