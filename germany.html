Assemblies Of God,Germany

The Assemblies of God-related Pentecostal movement in Germany is represented by the Bund Freikirchlicher Pfingstgemeinden (BFP). It is part of Germany's wider Pentecostal and free-church movement and maintains international connections through WAGF.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity began developing in Germany during the early twentieth century as the Pentecostal revival spread throughout Europe.


History

German Pentecostal churches developed through local congregations and independent ministries and eventually established national structures for cooperation, ministry and leadership. The BFP became an important national Pentecostal fellowship.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development, worship and practical Christian service.


Today

The Bund Freikirchlicher Pfingstgemeinden (BFP) is listed by WAGF as Germany's participating Pentecostal body, with its national leadership based in Erzhausen.
