Assemblies Of God, Burundi

The Assemblies of God presence in Burundi is represented within the wider Pentecostal Christian movement through Burundi Christian Life Ministries, which is listed by the World Assemblies of God Fellowship among its participating national bodies.


Origin

The movement developed within the growth o Pentecostal Christianity in Burundi, with Christian ministry centred on proclaiming the Gospel and developing local congregations.


History

Over time, Pentecostal churches and ministrie developed throughout Burundi, with local Christian leadership becoming central to the continuation and growth of the work.


Mission

The ministry focuses on sharing the Christian message, discipleship, leadership developmen fellowship and strengthening Christian communities.


Today

The Assemblies of God-related ministry in Burundi continues its work through Christian leaders and congregations seeking to serve th communities and proclaim the Gospel.
