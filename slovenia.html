Assemblies of God, Slovenia

The Assemblies of God presence in Slovenia is represented through the Union of Pentecostal Churches of Slovenia.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity in Slovenia developed within the wider European Pentecostal movement and became organized through local Pentecostal congregations.


History

The movement has developed churches and Christian ministry in Slovenia while maintaining relationships with the wider Pentecostal community.


Mission

Its mission is focused on proclaiming the Christian faith, developing disciples, strengthening churches and reaching local communities.


Today

The Union of Pentecostal Churches in Slovenia is represented within the World Assemblies of God Fellowship.
