Assemblies of God,Guyana

The Assemblies of God in Guyana is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through local churches, worship, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian ministry. It forms part of the wider international Assemblies of God fellowship.


Origin

The movement developed in Guyana during the spread of Pentecostal Christianity through the Caribbean and South America. Local Christian communities and ministers helped establish Pentecostal congregations and develop national leadership.


History

The Assemblies of God presence grew through churches, evangelistic work and the training of Christian workers. Over time, the movement developed a national structure known as the Assemblies of God in Guyana.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, planting and strengthening churches, training leaders and serving communities.


Today

The Assemblies of God in Guyana is listed by WAGF as the national Assemblies of God body for Guyana, with its leadership based in Georgetown.
