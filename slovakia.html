Assemblies of God, Slovakia

The Assemblies of God presence in Slovakia is represented by the Apostolic Church in Slovakia, a Pentecostal movement with churches and ministry centers throughout the country.


Origin

The movement's documented history includes the establishment of an independent church in 1932 and its development as an organized movement during the twentieth century.


History

The Apostolic Church became a movement in 1948 and faced restrictions under the communist regime before receiving state recognition in 1977. Following the restoration of greater religious freedom, its ministry expanded through evangelism, church planting, youth work and missions.


Mission

Its work includes preaching the Gospel, reaching young people, supporting disadvantaged communities, planting churches and training leaders.


Today

WAGF reports approximately 40 pastors, 30 churches, 20 preaching points and 5,000 believers. The movement also has ministry among Roma communities and participates in international missions.
