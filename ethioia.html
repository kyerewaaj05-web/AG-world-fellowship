Assemblies Of God,Ethiopia

The Ethiopia Assemblies of God is part of the worldwide Assemblies of God Pentecostal family. It carries out its ministry through churches, evangelism, discipleship, leadership development and church planting.


Origin

The Ethiopian Assemblies of God was formed in the late 1990s, according to the World Assemblies of God Fellowship. Its development took place within Ethiopia's rapidly growing Pentecostal Christian environment.


History

During its first two decades, the Ethiopian Assemblies of God established about 340 national churches. WAGF reports that a later emphasis on church planting and discipleship led to very rapid expansion, with the number of churches nearly tripling during the following four years.


Mission

Church planting and discipleship are central to the Ethiopian Assemblies of God's mission. Its model encourages established congregations to participate in planting new churches and developing new leaders.


Today

Ethiopia is one of the countries with the largest Assemblies of God populations in the world. WAGF's church-planting research lists 14,225,000 Assemblies of God adherents for Ethiopia in its 2022 global data. The national movement continues to emphasize church multiplication and discipleship.
