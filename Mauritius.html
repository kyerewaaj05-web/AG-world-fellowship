Assemblies of God, Mauritius

The Assemblies of God movement in Mauritius is a Pentecostal Christian presence serving communities through churches, worship, evangelism, discipleship and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Mauritius through the wider expansion of Pentecostal ministry in the Indian Ocean region.


History

Local congregations and Christian leaders helped establish and develop Pentecostal ministry on the island.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church development, prayer and Christian service.


Today

L'Assemblée de Dieu - Ile Maurice is listed by WAGF as an Assemblies of God body in Mauritius, with its leadership based in Rose-Hill.
