Assemblies Of God,Rwanda

The Rwanda Pentecostal Assemblies of God is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities throughout Rwanda. It is represented within the World Assemblies of God Fellowship and has a national leadership structure.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Rwanda developed as part of the wider Pentecostal expansion in East and Central Africa.


History

The movement grew through local Christian communities, evangelistic ministry and the development of national Christian leadership. Over time, Pentecostal churches became an important part of Rwanda's Christian landscape.


Mission

The church seeks to proclaim Jesus Christ, make disciples, establish and strengthen churches, develop leaders and serve communities.


Today

Rwanda Pentecostal Assemblies of God continues its ministry through congregations, Christian leadership and evangelistic activities, participating in the wider Assemblies of God family in Africa.
