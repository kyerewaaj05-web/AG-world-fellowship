Assemblies of God,Latvia

The Pentecostal movement in Latvia is part of the wider European Pentecostal family and maintains relationships with international Pentecostal organizations.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Latvia during the twentieth century, particularly during the broader spread of Pentecostal Christianity throughout Europe and the former Soviet region.


History

Pentecostal churches developed through local congregations and Christian workers. Assemblies of God ministry has also been connected with Pentecostal Bible-training activity in the region.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, prayer, church development and training Christian leaders.


Today

Pentecostal churches continue to serve Latvian communities, with the movement maintaining international Christian relationships.
