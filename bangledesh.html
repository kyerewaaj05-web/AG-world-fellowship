Assemblies of God,Bangledesh

The Assemblies of God in Bangladesh form part of the wider Pentecostal movement in South Asia. Churches and ministries serve communities through Christian worship, evangelism, discipleship, prayer, education and practical ministry.


Origin

Assemblies of God ministry in Bangladesh developed through Pentecostal missionary activity and the establishment of local Christian congregations. Over time, indigenous leaders and churches became increasingly involved in carrying the work forward.


History

Church ministry has included preaching, Bible teaching, prayer gatherings, children's ministry and leadership training.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, developing churches, training Christian leaders and praying for spiritual renewal and transformation.


Today

Bangladesh is recognized in the WAGF participating-country directory as a location represented within the worldwide Assemblies of God fellowship.
