Assemblies Of God,Zimbabwe

Assemblies of God in Zimbabwe is a Pentecostal Christian movement with a national presence in Zimbabwe. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship recognizes the General Council Assemblies of God in Zimbabwe and also lists Pentecostal Assemblies of Zimbabwe.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Zimbabwe developed within the broader Pentecostal movement in Southern Africa, with missionary ministry and local Christian leadership contributing to the establishment of churches.


History

The movement developed through evangelism, discipleship and the formation of local congregations. Over time, Zimbabwean ministers and believers assumed increasing responsibility for the work, allowing the church to develop its own national leadership and ministries.


Mission

The church seeks to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, plant and strengthen churches, develop leaders and participate in missions.


Today

AG Zimbabwe continues to carry out Christian ministry through local churches and leaders, seeking to serve communities and extend the Christian message.
