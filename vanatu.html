Assemblies of God, Vanuatu

The Assemblies of God of Vanuatu represents the Assemblies of God Pentecostal movement in Vanuatu.


Origin

The movement developed through Pentecostal Christian ministry in the Pacific and the establishment of Assemblies of God churches among Ni-Vanuatu communities.


History

Assemblies of God ministry has developed local churches and national leadership structures in Vanuatu.


Mission

Its mission is centered on Gospel proclamation, discipleship, church development, leadership and Christian service.


Today

The Assemblies of God of Vanuatu is represented in the World Assemblies of God Fellowship by its national leadership.
