Assemblies Of God,DR Congo

Assemblies of God ministry in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) is a significant Pentecostal presence. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship lists several recognized Assemblies of God bodies in the country, including the Communauté Assemblées de Dieu - Kinshasa, Congo Assemblies of God Fellowship, Assemblies of God - Kisangani, and 34ème Communauté des Assemblées de Dieu en Afrique.


Origin

Assemblies of God ministry in the DRC developed as Pentecostal Christianity spread through Central Africa. Missionary activity and the response of Congolese Christians contributed to the establishment of local congregations and ministries.


History

The movement developed across different regions of the country, with churches and Christian leadership emerging in major centres such as Kinshasa, Kisangani and South Kivu. The existence of several recognized Assemblies of God bodies reflects the diverse development of Pentecostal ministry within the country.


Mission

AG ministries in the DRC focus on evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development and Christian service.


Today

Assemblies of God churches continue to minister through congregations and Christian leaders across different parts of the DRC. The World AG Fellowship's recent church-planting research identifies the DRC among the countries with more than one million Assemblies of God adherents.
