Assemblies of God, El Salvador

The Assemblies of God of El Salvador is one of the established Pentecostal church movements in the country. It carries out ministry through churches, evangelism, discipleship, Christian education, leadership development and missions.


Origin

The Assemblies of God movement in El Salvador was established in 1930 through cooperation between Salvadoran Christian Francisco Ramírez Arbizu and Assemblies of God missionaries. WAGF records that Arbizu had travelled to San Antonio, Texas, in 1928 and subsequently helped introduce the new evangelical work to Assemblies of God leaders in the United States.


History

The early work was organized with approximately 100 members and 10 pastors. From that beginning, the movement expanded substantially, establishing churches and developing Christian educational and missionary ministries throughout the country. WAGF's published profile records approximately 1,700 organized churches, 2,200 affiliated pastors, 70 missionaries and 55 Christian schools.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, training ministers, Christian education and sending missionaries to other nations.


Today

The Conferencia Evangelica de las Asambleas de Dios continues to serve as the national Assemblies of God organization in El Salvador. Its listed national leadership is based in San Salvador.
