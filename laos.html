Assemblies of God,Laos

Assemblies of God Christians in Laos are part of the wider Pentecostal movement and serve through worship, evangelism, discipleship and Christian fellowship.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Laos as international and indigenous Christian movements spread through the country.


History

Assemblies of God congregations have existed in Laos alongside other Protestant communities. Historical U.S. government religious-freedom reports identified Assemblies of God among Christian groups


Mission

The ministry focuses on sharing the Christian faith, discipling believers and developing Christian communities.


Today

Assemblies of God churches remain part of the Christian landscape of Laos. Because the Lao government has historically placed restrictions on religious registration and activity, reliable current national membership figures are not readily available.
