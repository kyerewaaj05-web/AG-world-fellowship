Assemblies of God, Taiwan

: Assemblies of God presence in Taiwan is represented by the China Assemblies of God Taiwan District Council and associated Assemblies of God churches.


Origin

Pentecostal Assemblies of God ministry developed in Taiwan within the wider Christian and Pentecostal movement in East Asia.


History

The movement has established churches and national structures for Pentecostal ministry and Christian service.


Mission

Its mission includes Gospel proclamation, discipleship, church development and Christian ministry among local communities.


Today

WAGF lists the China Assemblies of God Taiwan District Council and identifies God's Love Assembly of God in Taipei within its Taiwan entry.
