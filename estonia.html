Assemblies of God,Estonia

The Assemblies of God/Pentecostal movement in Estonia forms part of the country's evangelical Christian community.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity reached Estonia through the wider Pentecostal movement in Europe and the work of Christian ministers and congregations.


History

The movement developed through local churches, evangelistic ministry, prayer and Bible teaching.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim Christ, disciple believers and strengthen Christian witness in Estonia


Today

Estonia belongs to the European Assemblies of God/Pentecostal network and participates in the wider international fellowship.
