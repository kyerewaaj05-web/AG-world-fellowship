Assemblies Of God,Republic Of Congo

The Assemblies of God in the Republic of the Congo is part of the Pentecostal Assemblies of God family and serves communities through churches, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian ministry.


Origin

The Assemblies of God movement developed in the Republic of the Congo through the wider spread of Pentecostal Christianity in Central Africa and the growth of national Christian leadership.


History

The national Assemblies of God presence has developed through local congregations and organized church bodies. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship currently lists more than one Assemblies of God-related national organization in the Republic of the Congo, including the Église des Assemblées de Dieu du Congo and the Evangelical Mission of the Assemblies of God of Congo (MEADC).


Mission

The churches seek to proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers, strengthen Christian communities and establish churches capable of continuing the work of evangelism and ministry.


Today

Assemblies of God ministry continues in the Republic of the Congo under national leadership, with organizations based in Brazzaville. The presence of multiple WAGF-listed bodies means the country's Assemblies of God story should not be presented as if it were controlled by only one national organization.
