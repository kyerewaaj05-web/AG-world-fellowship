Assemblies Of God,India

The Assemblies of God movement in India is a large Pentecostal Christian presence serving communities through churches, evangelism, discipleship, Christian education, leadership training and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity spread in India during the twentieth century through missionary activity and Indian Christian leaders. Assemblies of God churches subsequently developed in different parts of the country.


History

The movement developed national and regional ministries, with churches and Christian workers serving communities across India. The General Council of the Assemblies of God of India is listed by WAGF as an Assemblies of God body, while the WAGF directory also contains another Pentecostal organization connected with its wider fellowship.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, theological and ministerial education, leadership development and missions.


Today

The General Council of the Assemblies of God of India is listed by WAGF with national leadership based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Because India's Pentecostal landscape contains several distinct organizations, a country page should avoid presenting every Assemblies of God or Pentecostal congregation as one single institution.
