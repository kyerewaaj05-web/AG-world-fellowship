Assemblies of God,Equitorial Guinea

The Assemblies of God in Equatorial Guinea is represented by the Concilio General de las Asambleas de Dios de Guinea Ecuatorial.


Origin

According to WAGF, Assemblies of God ministry in Equatorial Guinea began in 1988.


History

The movement developed from its beginnings into an organized national church network with pastors and congregations.


Mission

Its mission is centered on evangelism, discipleship, church planting and Christian ministry.


Today

WAGF reports 118 churches, 122 pastors and approximately 18,200 adherents in Equatorial Guinea.
