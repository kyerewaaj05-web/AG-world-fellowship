Assemblies of God,Poland

The Pentecostal Church in Poland is part of the wider Pentecostal Assemblies of God family and serves communities through worship, preaching, discipleship, evangelism and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Poland during the twentieth century. WAGF historical material connects the development of Assemblies of God ministry in parts of Eastern Europe with Bible-training and missionary activity.


History

The movement developed local churches and national leadership while emphasizing Pentecostal worship and the ministry of the Holy Spirit.


Mission

Its stated priorities include maintaining Pentecostal spiritual life, caring for pastors, strengthening local churches, planting new churches and preparing younger ministers for future ministry.


Today

WAGF's profile records approximately 230 churches and 23,000 believers in the Pentecostal Church in Poland.
