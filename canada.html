Assemblies Of God,Canada

The Assemblies of God movement in Canada is represented by the General Conference of the Canadian Assemblies of God. It is part of the international Pentecostal fellowship and serves communities through churches, preaching, discipleship, worship, leadership development and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Canada during the early twentieth century alongside the wider Pentecostal revival. Over time, Pentecostal churches developed national organizations and networks.


History

The Canadian Assemblies of God movement has developed alongside other Pentecostal fellowships in the country. WAGF also separately lists the Pentecostal Assemblies of Newfoundland and Labrador, demonstrating that Assemblies of God-related Pentecostal bodies in Canada


Mission

Its mission includes preaching the Gospel, making disciples, establishing healthy churches, training leaders and participating in missions locally and internationally.


Today

The General Conference of the Canadian Assemblies of God is represented within the World Assemblies of God Fellowship, with its listed national office in Brantford, Ontario. WAGF separately reports the Pentecostal Assemblies of Newfoundland and Labrador as a participating body.
