Assemblies Of God,Guinea

The Assemblies of God in Guinea is a Pentecostal Christian movement committed to proclaiming the Gospel and establishing communities of Christian faith. The national work includes churches, pastors and Bible training, providing a foundation for evangelism and discipleship.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Guinea developed through Pentecostal missionary activity and the establishment of local congregations dedicated to evangelism and Christian teaching.


History

The movement has developed a network of churches and preaching points and has also established Bible-training work for pastors and Christian workers. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship describes the Guinean work as growing and records a national presence including churches, pastors and a Bible school.


Mission

The mission is centred on evangelism, discipleship, church development and training people for Christian ministry.


Today

AG Guinea continues to develop its churches and leadership while seeking to reach additional communities with the Christian message.
