Assemblies of God,Argentina

The Assemblies of God in Argentina is part of the worldwide Pentecostal Assemblies of God family. Its national church movement serves communities through local congregations, evangelism, discipleship, Christian education and missions.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity reached Argentina during the early twentieth century. The wider Pentecostal movement developed through missionaries, evangelists and local believers, eventually producing organized Pentecostal denominations and national church structures. Historical WAGF material records early Pentecostal activity in Argentina beginning in the first decade of the twentieth century.


History

As Pentecostal Christianity expanded, churches developed throughout the country and increasingly relied on national Christian leadership. The Assemblies of God in Argentina is represented internationally through the Union de las Asambleas de Dios.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, planting and strengthening churches, training Christian leaders and participating in missions.


Today

The Unión de las Asambleas de Dios continues to represent the Assemblies of God movement in Argentina within the global fellowship, with national leadership based in Buenos Aires.
