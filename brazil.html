Assemblies Of God,Brazil

The Assemblies of God in Brazil constitute one of the major national expressions of the worldwide Assemblies of God movement.


Origin

Assemblies of God ministry began in Brazil in the early twentieth century through the work of Swedish Pentecostal missionaries Daniel Berg and Gunnar Vingren.


History

The movement spread from its earliest northern Brazilian centers into other parts of the country. Churches multiplied as Brazilian pastors and evangelists carried Pentecostal teaching and ministry into new communities.


Growth & Development

Brazilian Assemblies of God churches have developed extensive networks of congregations, Bible institutes, missions organizations, schools and ministries serving children, youth and families.


Mission

Evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development and missions remain important parts of its ministry.
