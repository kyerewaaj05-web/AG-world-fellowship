Assemblies Of God,Angola

The Angola Assemblies of God is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities in Angola. It is recognized as the national Assemblies of God body in Angola by the World Assemblies of God Fellowship.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Angola developed within the wider Pentecostal missionary movement in Central and Southern Africa. The Gospel was carried into communities through missionary activity and the work of local Christian believers.


History

The movement developed through local congregations, evangelism and Christian leadership. As churches became established, Angolan believers and ministers played an increasingly important role in the continuation of the work.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development and Christian service.


Today

Angola Assemblies of God continues to work through churches and Christian leaders, seeking to reach communities with the Gospel and strengthen believers in their faith. Its national leadership is based in Luanda.
