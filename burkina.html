Assemblies Of God,Burkina Faso

Assemblies of God in Burkina Faso is a major Pentecostal Christian movement whose ministry includes evangelism, church planting, discipleship, leadership development and missions. The national church is known as the Église des Assemblées de Dieu du Burkina Faso.


Origin

The Assemblies of God movement in Burkina Faso grew out of the wider Pentecostal missionary movement in West Africa. The country subsequently became an important centre for Assemblies of God ministry and missions in the region.


History

The church developed through local congregations and indigenous Christian leadership. Its missionary vision also became an important part of its history. A World Assemblies of God Fellowship missions document records that pastors from three local churches met in Ouagadougou in April 1984 to launch the Burkina Faso Assemblies of God Missions Department.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development and sending missionaries to other communities and nations.


Today

AG Burkina Faso continues to participate in both national and international mission, making it an important part of the Assemblies of God movement in West Africa.
