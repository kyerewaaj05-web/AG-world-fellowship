Assemblies Of God,South Korea

Assemblies of God churches in South Korea are represented by several Pentecostal organizations, including Assemblies of God Korea, Assemblies of God Korea (GwangHwaMun), and Jesus Assemblies of God in Korea.


Origin

The Assemblies of God movement in Korea developed within the country's wider Pentecostal and evangelical Christian movement.


History

Korean Assemblies of God churches have developed major congregational, evangelistic, educational and leadership ministries.


Mission

Their mission centers on Gospel proclamation, Spirit-filled Christian life, discipleship, church ministry and reaching Korean society.


Today

WAGF recognizes multiple Assemblies of God organizations in South Korea, rather than presenting the country's Pentecostal Assemblies of God presence as a single organization.
