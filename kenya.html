Assemblies Of God,Kenya

Assemblies of God in Kenya is a Pentecostal Christian movement committed to proclaiming Jesus Christ, developing disciples and establishing strong Christian communities. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship recognizes national Assemblies of God bodies in Kenya, including Kenya Assemblies of God and Pentecostal Assemblies of God of Kenya.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Kenya developed through the wider Pentecostal missionary movement in East Africa. Over time, missionary activity combined with the work of Kenyan believers and ministers to establish local congregations and national church structures.


History

As the movement developed, churches were established in different communities and Kenyan leaders increasingly carried responsibility for ministry and church development. Kenya subsequently became one of the significant Assemblies of God contexts in Africa, with the World AG Fellowship identifying more than one recognized AG body in the country.


Mission

The church's mission centres on evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development and missions.


Today

AG ministry in Kenya continues through churches, pastors, evangelists, Christian education and missionary activity, seeking to reach communities and equip believers for Christian service.
