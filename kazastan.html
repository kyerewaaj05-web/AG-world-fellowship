Assemblies of God,Kazakhstan

The Assemblies of God in Kazakhstan is part of the wider Pentecostal Christian family and serves communities through churches, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and Christian teaching.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Kazakhstan during the twentieth century, particularly as Pentecostal and evangelical movements expanded across the former Soviet region.


History

Local Christian congregations and leaders developed Pentecostal ministry within Kazakhstan. Over time, organized national structures emerged to support churches and Christian workers.


Mission

Its mission includes proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, establishing and strengthening churches, training Christian leaders and serving communities.


Today

WAGF lists Assemblies of God in Kazakhstan as one participating Pentecostal body, alongside the Union of Christians of Evangelical Faith in Kazakhstan. The Assemblies of God body is listed with leadership based in Almaty.
