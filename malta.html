Origin

The Assemblies of God of Malta is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities through worship, preaching, evangelism, discipleship and Christian ministry.


History

The movement developed local congregations and national leadership. WAGF records Rev. Joseph Fenech Laudi as having served as the organization's first superintendent from 1992-1994 and later being reappointed in 2011.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, disciple believers, strengthen churches and equip Christians for service.


Today

The General Council of the Assemblies of God of Malta is listed by WAGF, with leadership associated with Cornerstone Pentecostal Church in Pembroke.
