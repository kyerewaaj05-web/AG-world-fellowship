Assemblies Of God, USA

The Assemblies of God in the United States is represented by the General Council of the Assemblies of God, one of the major countries listed by WAGF


Origin

Assemblies of God movement in United States emerged from the early twentieth-century Pentecostal movement and developed into a structured national fellowship.


History

The movement expanded through evangelism, church planting, Bible education, missions and the development of local congregations across the United States.


Mission

The General Council describes its mission as evangelizing the lost, worshiping God, discipling believers and demonstrating God's love through compassion.


Today

WAGF reports more than 3 million adherents in over 12,800 churches in the United States. It also reports nearly 37,000 credentialed pastors and ministry leaders and more than 2,800 missionaries serving internationally.
