Assemblies Of God,Eswatini

Assemblies of God ministry in Eswatini is part of the Pentecostal Christian movement and seeks to serve people through worship, biblical teaching, evangelism and Christian fellowship.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Eswatini developed within the Pentecostal missionary movement in Southern Africa.


History

The work developed through local churches and Christian ministries, with national leadership helping coordinate the movement's activities. The World Assemblies of God Fellowship lists the International Ministries Assemblies of God in Eswatini among its participating bodies.


Mission

The church seeks to reach people with the Gospel, disciple believers, develop Christian leaders and strengthen local congregations.


Today

AG ministry in Eswatini continues through churches and Christian ministries seeking to serve communities and advance the Christian faith.
