Assemblies Of God,Lesotho

The Lesotho Assemblies of God is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities in the Kingdom of Lesotho. It forms part of the wider Assemblies of God fellowship and is committed to Christian worship, biblical teaching, evangelism and discipleship.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Lesotho developed as part of the wider Pentecostal missionary expansion in Southern Africa.


History

The movement gradually established a national church presence, with local congregations and ministers carrying out evangelistic and pastoral ministry. The national body is represented within the World Assemblies of God Fellowship.


Mission

Its mission is to proclaim the Gospel, develop mature Christian believers, establish churches and equip people for ministry.


Today

The Lesotho Assemblies of God continues to serve through local churches, pastoral ministry, evangelism and Christian discipleship.
