Assemblies Of God, South Africa

Assemblies of God ministry in South Africa is represented by multiple Assemblies of God organizations, including the Assemblies of God of South Africa, South Africa Assemblies of God Fellowship and International Assemblies of God of South Africa.


Origin

Assemblies of God Pentecostal ministry developed in South Africa as part of the wider international Pentecostal movement.


History

The movement has developed through churches, ministers and national fellowships serving different communities across South Africa.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, Christian leadership and community ministry.


Today

WAGF lists multiple Assemblies of God bodies in South Africa, demonstrating that the country's Assemblies of God presence is represented by more than one organization rather than a single national body.
