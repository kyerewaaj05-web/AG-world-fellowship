Assemblies of God,Georgia

The Pentecostal movement in Georgia is represented within the wider international Pentecostal family. WAGF lists the Georgia Pentecostal Union as the participating Pentecostal body for the Republic of Georgia.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Georgia during the twentieth century as Pentecostal teaching and ministry spread through the region.


History

Local Pentecostal congregations and Christian leaders developed the movement within Georgia, eventually forming organized national structures. The movement has continued to operate within the country's Christian community while maintaining international Pentecostal relationships.


Mission

Its mission includes preaching the Gospel, discipleship, prayer, Christian fellowship and establishing churches capable of serving their communities.


Today

The Georgia Pentecostal Union is listed by WAGF with its leadership based in Tbilisi
