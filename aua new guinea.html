Assemblies of God,Papua New Guinea

The Assemblies of God of Papua New Guinea is a Pentecostal Christian movement serving communities throughout the country through churches, evangelism, discipleship, worship and Christian ministry.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Papua New Guinea through missionary activity and the growth of indigenous Christian leadership.


History

Assemblies of God churches developed among Papua New Guinea's many communities and languages, with national structures eventually established to support church ministry and leadership.


Mission

Its mission includes evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development and missions.


Today

The Assemblies of God of Papua New Guinea is listed by WAGF as the national Assemblies of God body, with leadership based in Boroko.
