Assemblies of God, Ukraine

The Ukrainian Pentecostal Church represents a major Pentecostal Christian movement in Ukraine and is included in the World Assemblies of God Fellowship.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity developed in Ukraine through evangelistic and church-planting movements that later produced extensive national church structures.


History

The Ukrainian Pentecostal movement has developed a broad network of churches, pastors, social ministries, children's ministries, media work and missionary activity.


Mission

Its mission includes Gospel proclamation, discipleship, youth ministry, social care, rehabilitation work, church development and international missions.


Today

WAGF reports more than 1,510 Pentecostal churches and approximately 104,000 members, with more than 1,300 pastors and 1,600 deacons. The movement also operates rehabilitation centers, orphanages, elderly-care homes and prison ministries and has sent missionaries abroad.
