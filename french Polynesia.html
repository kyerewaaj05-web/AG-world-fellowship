Assemblies of God, French Polynesia

The Assemblées de Dieu de Polynésie Française is the Pentecostal fellowship in French Polynesia affiliated with the World Assemblies of God Fellowship, serving churches across Tahiti, Moorea, and the wider territory.


Origin

Pentecostal Christianity reached the Pacific through missionary activity and subsequently developed indigenous churches.


History

Local Christian communities developed through evangelism, worship, discipleship and pastoral ministry.


Mission

The mission is to proclaim the Gospel and strengthen churches throughout the islands.


Today

Churches have focused on local evangelism, youth and family ministry, Bible teaching and leadership development. French Polynesia belongs within the Pacific/Oceania portion of the global Assemblies of God story. The national fellowship is headquartered in Papeete, Tahiti.
