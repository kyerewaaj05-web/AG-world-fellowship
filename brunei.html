Assemblies of God,Brunei

The Assemblies of God in Brunei is part of the worldwide Assemblies of God Pentecostal fellowship. Its ministry serves Christians through worship, biblical teaching, prayer, fellowship, discipleship and evangelism.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Brunei developed within the wider expansion of Pentecostal Christianity in Southeast Asia. The national body is represented internationally through the Brunei Assemblies of God General Council.


History

The movement has developed its ministry within Brunei's distinctive religious and cultural environment, with emphasis on maintaining Christian fellowship and strengthening believers.


Mission

Its mission centers on proclaiming the Christian faith, making disciples, strengthening local Christian communities and equipping believers for service.


Today

The Brunei Assemblies of God General Council is listed by the World Assemblies of God Fellowship as the national Assemblies of God body in Brunei, with leadership based in Bandar Seri Begawan.
