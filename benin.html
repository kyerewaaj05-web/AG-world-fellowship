Assemblies Of God,Benin

Assemblies of God in Benin is a Pentecostal Christian movement committed to proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ, developing Christian believers and establishing churches throughout the country. The national church is known as the Église Évangélique des Assemblées de Dieu du Bénin and is part of the wider World Assemblies of God Fellowship.


Origin

The Assemblies of God presence in Benin developed within the wider Pentecostal missionary movement in West Africa. Over time, Christian workers and local believers established congregations and developed a national church structure.


History

The church's history is connected to the broader development of Pentecostal Christianity in French-speaking West Africa. As congregations developed, local Christian leadership became increasingly important in carrying the work forward. Benin is also connected to regional Assemblies of God ministry and leadership development.


Mission

The church focuses on evangelism, discipleship, church planting, Christian leadership and the strengthening of local congregations.


Today

Assemblies of God in Benin continues to serve through local churches, Christian ministry and evangelistic work, seeking to reach communities and develop believers.
